# The Complete Marvel Watch Guide

> **Current as of November 8, 2026.** Covers every Marvel Studios film, the Marvel television shows that matter to the main story, the Spider-Man and Venom films (Sony), the X-Men films (20th Century Fox), and every Fantastic Four film.

---

## How to use this guide

- **New to Marvel?** Watch the films in **Part 1** in the order listed (release order). That is how audiences experienced the story, and it keeps every surprise and every after-credits tease intact.
- **Want the shows too?** Part 2 lists each show along with the film it follows. Slot the shows in at the point named in each entry (the **Master Watch Order** at the end of this guide does this for you).
- **Spider-Man, Venom, X-Men, Fantastic Four:** each has its own section with its own full list, story summaries and a chart showing how its films connect to the main story.
- **Spoilers:** every summary covers the full storyline, including the ending, and lists every scene hidden in or after the credits. If you want to go in blind, read only the "Released" line and skip the rest.
- **Credit scenes:** "Mid-credits" means a scene that plays after the first set of stylized credits. "Post-credits" means a scene at the very end, after all of the scrolling credits. Marvel almost always has at least one, and they often set up the next film, so **always stay until the very end**.

### Terms used in this guide

| Term | What it means |
|---|---|
| Marvel Cinematic Universe | The shared world of the Marvel Studios films and shows, starting with *Iron Man* (2008). |
| Phase | Marvel Studios groups its releases into "Phases." Each Phase ends (or peaks) in a major team-up. |
| The Infinity Saga | Phases 1, 2 and 3 (2008 to 2019): the story of Thanos and the six Infinity Stones. |
| The Multiverse Saga | Phases 4, 5 and 6 (2021 to 2027): the story of parallel universes, building to *Avengers: Doomsday* and *Avengers: Secret Wars*. |
| Infinity Stones | Six gems of limitless power (Space, Mind, Reality, Power, Time, Soul). Thanos wants all six. |
| Multiverse | The idea that infinite parallel universes exist. Universes are given numbers, like "Earth-616" (the main Marvel Cinematic Universe) or "Earth-828" (the Fantastic Four's world in *First Steps*). |
| S.H.I.E.L.D. | The Strategic Homeland Intervention, Enforcement and Logistics Division: the spy agency, run by Nick Fury, that forms the Avengers. |
| Time Variance Authority | A bureaucracy outside of time that polices the timeline (introduced in the *Loki* show). |
| Disney+ | Disney's streaming service, where nearly every film and show in this guide streams. |

# Part 1: The Avengers Films

## 1.1 Every Marvel Studios Film, in Release Order

> Watch these top to bottom and you will not miss a single piece of the Avengers story. "Set in" is the year the story takes place inside the universe, which sometimes differs from the release year.

## THE INFINITY SAGA

---

### PHASE 1: Avengers Assemble (2008 to 2012)

---

#### 1. Iron Man (2008)
**Released:** May 2, 2008 · **Phase 1** · **Set in:** 2008 (Marvel's 2023 official timeline; older charts say 2010)

**Story:** Tony Stark, a genius billionaire weapons maker, is ambushed and kidnapped in Afghanistan by the Ten Rings terrorist group, who order him to build them a missile. Instead, with fellow prisoner Ho Yinsen, he builds an arc reactor to keep shrapnel out of his heart and a crude armored suit to escape. Back home, shaken by seeing his own weapons used against innocent people, Tony shuts down Stark Industries' weapons division and secretly perfects a sleek, flying Iron Man suit. He discovers that his business partner Obadiah Stane has been selling weapons to terrorists and arranged the kidnapping; Stane steals Tony's arc reactor to power his own giant suit, the Iron Monger, and Tony defeats him with help from his assistant Pepper Potts. At a press conference the next day, Tony throws away his cover story and announces, "I am Iron Man." The film introduces S.H.I.E.L.D. through Agent Phil Coulson.

**Credit scenes:**
- **Post-credits:** Nick Fury (Samuel L. Jackson) appears in Tony's living room and tells him he is not the only superhero in the world. He's here to talk about "the Avengers Initiative."

---

#### 2. The Incredible Hulk (2008)
**Released:** June 13, 2008 · **Phase 1** · **Set in:** 2010 (the same week as *Iron Man 2* and *Thor*)
*(Distributed by Universal Pictures, which is why it was missing from Disney+ for years; it returned to Disney in 2023.)*

**Story:** Scientist Bruce Banner (played here by Edward Norton; Mark Ruffalo takes over the role from *The Avengers* onward) is hiding in Brazil after a gamma radiation experiment turned him into the Hulk, a rage-fuelled green giant. General Thaddeus "Thunderbolt" Ross wants to capture him and turn the Hulk into a weapon. Banner returns to the United States hoping for a cure and reunites with Betty Ross, the general's daughter and the woman he loves. Ross injects soldier Emil Blonsky with an early version of the super soldier serum and then Banner's blood, turning him into the monstrous Abomination, who tears through Harlem until the Hulk defeats him. Banner escapes and is last seen learning to control his transformations. General Ross and the Abomination both return years later (*Captain America: Brave New World* and *She-Hulk*), so this film matters more than people expect.

**Credit scenes:**
- **Final scene (just before the credits):** Tony Stark walks into a bar and tells a drunk General Ross that "we're putting a team together." There is no scene after the credits.

---

#### 3. Iron Man 2 (2010)
**Released:** May 7, 2010 · **Phase 1** · **Set in:** 2010

**Story:** Six months after revealing he is Iron Man, Tony is resisting the United States government's demand to hand over his suits, while the palladium in his arc reactor is slowly poisoning him. Ivan Vanko, a Russian physicist whose father was cheated by Tony's father Howard, attacks Tony at a Monaco race with electrified whips, and rival arms dealer Justin Hammer hires Vanko to build him an army of drone suits. Tony, believing he is dying, spirals and makes Pepper the chief executive of his company. Nick Fury and undercover agent Natasha Romanoff (Black Widow, in her first appearance) help him: a message hidden in Howard Stark's old Stark Expo model shows Tony how to build a new element that cures him. Tony and his best friend James "Rhodey" Rhodes (now War Machine) defeat Vanko's drones together at the Stark Expo.

**Credit scenes:**
- **Post-credits:** Agent Coulson arrives at a desert crater in New Mexico and reports that "we've found it." The camera reveals Thor's hammer, Mjolnir, in the crater. *(The Incredible Hulk, Iron Man 2 and Thor all happen in roughly the same week.)*

---

#### 4. Thor (2011)
**Released:** May 6, 2011 · **Phase 1** · **Set in:** 2010

**Story:** Thor, the arrogant heir to the throne of Asgard (a realm of god-like beings), defies his father Odin by attacking the Frost Giants of Jotunheim. Odin strips him of his powers and banishes him to Earth, sending his hammer Mjolnir after him with an enchantment that only someone "worthy" can lift it. In New Mexico, Thor is found by astrophysicist Jane Foster, her mentor Erik Selvig and her intern Darcy, while S.H.I.E.L.D. seizes the hammer. Back in Asgard, Thor's adopted brother Loki learns he is actually a Frost Giant, takes the throne while Odin sleeps, and sends the Destroyer armor to kill Thor. Thor sacrifices himself to protect the townspeople, which makes him worthy again; he regains his powers, returns to Asgard, and stops Loki, who falls into the void of space. The bridge between worlds (the Bifrost) is destroyed, cutting Thor off from Jane. Hawkeye (Clint Barton) makes a brief first appearance.

**Credit scenes:**
- **Post-credits:** Nick Fury shows Erik Selvig a glowing blue cube, the Tesseract, and asks him to study it. An invisible Loki is revealed to be controlling Selvig's mind. *(The Tesseract holds the Space Stone, the first Infinity Stone shown in the films.)*

---

#### 5. Captain America: The First Avenger (2011)
**Released:** July 22, 2011 · **Phase 1** · **Set in:** 1942 to 1945 (with a final scene in the present day)

**Story:** During World War II, Steve Rogers, a frail but brave young man from Brooklyn, is rejected by the army again and again until Dr. Abraham Erskine chooses him for a super soldier experiment that turns him into a peak human being. After being used as a costumed propaganda mascot, Steve becomes the real hero Captain America when he rescues captured soldiers, including his best friend Bucky Barnes. With Agent Peggy Carter, Colonel Chester Phillips, Howard Stark and his elite team the Howling Commandos, Steve battles HYDRA, a rogue Nazi science division led by Johann Schmidt, the Red Skull, who powers his weapons with the Tesseract. Bucky falls from a train and is presumed dead. Steve stops the Red Skull's bomber from destroying American cities by crashing it into the Arctic ice; the Red Skull vanishes when he touches the Tesseract. Steve wakes up nearly 70 years later in modern-day New York, where Nick Fury tells him he has been asleep for decades.

**Credit scenes:**
- **Post-credits:** Nick Fury asks Steve to join a mission to save the world, followed by a short teaser trailer for *The Avengers*.

---

#### 6. The Avengers (2012)
**Released:** May 4, 2012 · **Phase 1** · **Set in:** 2012
*(Released as "Marvel Avengers Assemble" in the United Kingdom and Ireland.)*

**Story:** Loki returns to Earth through the Tesseract, mind-controls Hawkeye and Erik Selvig with a glowing scepter, and steals the cube. He has struck a deal with a mysterious alien power: he will hand over the Tesseract in exchange for an alien army, the Chitauri, to conquer Earth. Nick Fury assembles Iron Man, Captain America, Thor, Black Widow, the Hulk (now Mark Ruffalo) and Hawkeye, but the heroes clash with each other aboard S.H.I.E.L.D.'s flying Helicarrier until Loki kills Agent Coulson, which unites them. In the Battle of New York, the team fights the Chitauri invasion as Loki opens a portal in the sky over Manhattan; Tony flies a nuclear missile through the portal into space, destroying the alien mothership, and falls back to Earth just before the portal closes. Loki is captured and Thor takes him and the Tesseract back to Asgard. The near-death experience in space haunts Tony for years.

**Credit scenes:**
- **Mid-credits:** The Other, Loki's alien handler, reports to his master that attacking Earth means "to court death." The master turns and smiles: it is **Thanos**, in his first appearance.
- **Post-credits:** The exhausted Avengers eat shawarma in silence in a wrecked restaurant.

### PHASE 2: The Stones Emerge (2013 to 2015)

---

#### 7. Iron Man 3 (2013)
**Released:** May 3, 2013 · **Phase 2** · **Set in:** Christmas, a few months after *The Avengers* (Marvel's 2023 official timeline places it in late 2013, after *Thor: The Dark World*)

**Story:** Suffering from panic attacks and insomnia after the Battle of New York, Tony builds dozens of Iron Man suits to cope. A terrorist called the Mandarin is bombing targets across the world, and when Tony publicly threatens him, his Malibu mansion is destroyed. Stranded in rural Tennessee with a damaged suit, Tony befriends a kid named Harley Keener and discovers the bombings are caused by Extremis, a treatment that makes people regenerate but can make them explode. The real villain is scientist Aldrich Killian, whom Tony snubbed years earlier, and the "Mandarin" is a hired actor named Trevor Slattery. Tony and Rhodey rescue the kidnapped President and Pepper (who has been injected with Extremis) in a battle at an oil rig, using every one of his remote suits. Tony has Pepper cured, gets the shrapnel removed from his chest, and destroys all his suits, declaring that he is still Iron Man without them.

**Credit scenes:**
- **Post-credits:** It turns out Tony has been telling this whole story to Bruce Banner, who fell asleep listening.

---

#### 8. Thor: The Dark World (2013)
**Released:** November 8, 2013 · **Phase 2** · **Set in:** 2013

**Story:** An ancient enemy, Malekith of the Dark Elves, awakens when the Nine Realms align, seeking a fluid-like weapon called the Aether to plunge the universe back into darkness. Jane Foster accidentally absorbs the Aether in London, and Thor brings her to Asgard to save her. The Dark Elves attack Asgard and kill Thor's mother, Frigga. Desperate, Thor frees the imprisoned Loki for help; Loki appears to die saving Thor in battle. In a chaotic final fight in Greenwich, London, where gateways between worlds open and close, Thor defeats Malekith. Thor turns down his father's throne to be with Jane. In the last shot, "Odin" on the throne is revealed to be Loki in disguise.

**Credit scenes:**
- **Mid-credits:** Sif and Volstagg give the Aether to the Collector (Taneleer Tivan), explaining it is unsafe to keep two Infinity Stones in Asgard. After they leave, the Collector says, "One down, five to go." *(The Aether is the Reality Stone.)*
- **Post-credits:** Thor returns to Earth and kisses Jane. Meanwhile, a Frost Monster left behind from the battle is still running around London.

---

#### 9. Captain America: The Winter Soldier (2014)
**Released:** April 4, 2014 · **Phase 2** · **Set in:** 2014

**Story:** Steve Rogers works for S.H.I.E.L.D. in Washington D.C. but is uneasy about its plans to launch three new Helicarriers that can kill threats before they act. After Nick Fury is attacked and seemingly killed, Steve, Natasha and new ally Sam Wilson (the Falcon) discover that HYDRA has been secretly growing inside S.H.I.E.L.D. since World War II, led by senior official Alexander Pierce. HYDRA's top assassin, the Winter Soldier, turns out to be Bucky Barnes, brainwashed and kept frozen between missions for 70 years. The heroes reprogram the Helicarriers to destroy each other before HYDRA can use them, and Natasha dumps all of S.H.I.E.L.D.'s secrets online, effectively ending the agency. Steve refuses to fight Bucky and is pulled from a river by him, a sign Bucky remembers something. Fury, who faked his death, goes underground.

**Credit scenes:**
- **Mid-credits:** HYDRA leader Baron Strucker studies Loki's scepter in a lab and holds two "miracles" captive: the twins Pietro and Wanda Maximoff (Quicksilver and Scarlet Witch), in their first appearance.
- **Post-credits:** Bucky visits the Captain America exhibit at the Smithsonian museum and sees his own past.

---

#### 10. Guardians of the Galaxy (2014)
**Released:** August 1, 2014 · **Phase 2** · **Set in:** 2014

**Story:** Peter Quill, who was abducted from Earth as a boy in 1988 by a band of space pirates called the Ravagers, steals a mysterious orb on a distant planet. Every bounty hunter in the galaxy wants it, and he ends up in prison with green assassin Gamora (Thanos's adopted daughter), the genetically engineered raccoon Rocket, the tree-like Groot, and the vengeful warrior Drax. They escape together and learn the orb contains an Infinity Stone (the Power Stone) that the fanatical Kree warrior Ronan the Accuser plans to use, after betraying Thanos, to destroy the planet Xandar. The misfits become a team, and in the final battle Groot sacrifices himself to protect them, while Quill holds the Power Stone together with his friends' help to destroy Ronan. They give the stone to the Nova Corps for safe keeping, and a sprout of Groot is replanted as Baby Groot. Quill learns his father was "not from Earth."

**Credit scenes:**
- **Mid-credits:** Baby Groot dances in his pot to the Jackson 5, freezing whenever Drax looks at him.
- **Post-credits:** In his destroyed museum, the Collector sits with Cosmo the space dog and is mocked by Howard the Duck.

---

#### 11. Avengers: Age of Ultron (2015)
**Released:** May 1, 2015 · **Phase 2** · **Set in:** 2015

**Story:** The Avengers raid a HYDRA base and recover Loki's scepter, which Tony and Bruce discover holds an artificial intelligence. Haunted by a vision of his friends dying, Tony uses it to secretly build Ultron, a peacekeeping program meant to protect the world, but Ultron decides humanity must go extinct. Ultron recruits the Maximoff twins, whose powers came from the scepter: Wanda's mind tricks give each Avenger nightmares and send the Hulk on a rampage in Johannesburg, which Tony stops with his giant Hulkbuster armor. The team regroups at Hawkeye's secret family farm, and Ultron's plan to build a new body backfires when the Avengers take it, combining Tony's assistant program JARVIS with the gem from the scepter (the Mind Stone) to create **Vision**. Ultron lifts the city of Sokovia into the sky to crash it like a meteor; the team evacuates the city, Pietro dies saving Hawkeye, and Vision destroys Ultron's last body. Afterwards, the Hulk flies off alone into space, Thor leaves to investigate the Infinity Stones, and Captain America and Black Widow train a new team: War Machine, Falcon, Scarlet Witch and Vision.

**Credit scenes:**
- **Mid-credits:** Thanos opens a vault, puts on the golden Infinity Gauntlet, and says, "Fine. I'll do it myself."
- **Post-credits:** None.

---

#### 12. Ant-Man (2015)
**Released:** July 17, 2015 · **Phase 2** · **Set in:** 2015

**Story:** Scott Lang, a good-hearted burglar just out of prison who wants to be a better father to his daughter Cassie, is recruited by scientist Hank Pym, the original Ant-Man from the 1960s to 1980s. Pym's former protégé Darren Cross has nearly recreated Pym's shrinking technology and plans to sell a weaponized version, the Yellowjacket suit, to HYDRA. Hank and his daughter Hope van Dyne train Scott to use the Ant-Man suit, which shrinks him to insect size while making him stronger, and to control ants. Scott briefly fights Falcon while stealing a part from the new Avengers base. During the heist, Scott shrinks to sub-atomic size to defeat Cross, enters the "Quantum Realm" where Hank's wife Janet was lost decades earlier, and manages to come back, giving Hank hope that Janet could still be alive.

**Credit scenes:**
- **Mid-credits:** Hank shows Hope a new prototype Wasp suit that was meant for her mother, and Hope says, "It's about damn time."
- **Post-credits:** Steve and Sam have Bucky pinned in a hidden location and say they cannot call Tony because of "the Accords." Sam says he might "know a guy" (meaning Scott). This is a scene from *Captain America: Civil War*.

### PHASE 3: The Infinity War (2016 to 2019)

---

#### 13. Captain America: Civil War (2016)
**Released:** May 6, 2016 · **Phase 3** · **Set in:** 2016

**Story:** After a mission in Lagos goes wrong and civilians die, the world's governments demand the Avengers sign the Sokovia Accords, which would put them under United Nations control. Tony, guilt-ridden over Ultron, signs; Steve refuses. When a bombing at the United Nations is blamed on Bucky, Steve protects him, splitting the team into two sides that fight at a German airport: Tony's side includes Black Widow, War Machine, Vision, the newly introduced Black Panther (T'Challa, prince of Wakanda, whose father died in the bombing) and a teenage Spider-Man (Peter Parker, recruited by Tony); Steve's side includes Falcon, Bucky, Hawkeye, Scarlet Witch and Ant-Man. The real mastermind, Helmut Zemo, a Sokovian who lost his family in Ultron's attack, lures them to a Siberian HYDRA base and shows Tony footage proving a brainwashed Bucky killed Tony's parents in 1991. Tony attacks in fury, Steve and Bucky defeat him, and Steve leaves his shield behind. The Avengers are broken: Steve's team become fugitives, and Steve breaks his imprisoned friends out of the Raft prison.

**Credit scenes:**
- **Mid-credits:** In Wakanda, Bucky chooses to go back into frozen sleep until a cure for his brainwashing can be found, while T'Challa shelters him.
- **Post-credits:** Peter Parker finds that his new web-shooters, a gift from Tony, project a red Spider-Man symbol. Text reads: "Spider-Man will return."

---

#### 14. Doctor Strange (2016)
**Released:** November 4, 2016 · **Phase 3** · **Set in:** 2016 to 2017

**Story:** Stephen Strange, an arrogant and brilliant neurosurgeon, crushes his hands in a car crash and spends his fortune seeking a cure. In Kathmandu, Nepal, he finds the Ancient One, a sorcerer who teaches him the mystic arts, and her student Karl Mordo. Strange learns that the Eye of Agamotto, an amulet that controls time, holds an Infinity Stone (the Time Stone). Former student Kaecilius tries to bring Earth into the Dark Dimension, ruled by the demon Dormammu. After the Ancient One dies, Strange traps Dormammu in a time loop, dying over and over until Dormammu agrees to leave Earth in exchange for being released. Strange becomes the protector of the New York Sanctum, while Mordo, disgusted at how the rules of nature were bent, walks away.

**Credit scenes:**
- **Mid-credits:** Thor visits Strange in New York, looking for his father Odin, and Strange agrees to help find him if Thor takes Loki back to Asgard. *(This scene plays out again in *Thor: Ragnarok*.)*
- **Post-credits:** Mordo steals the magic powers of Jonathan Pangborn and declares the world has "too many sorcerers."

---

#### 15. Guardians of the Galaxy Vol. 2 (2017)
**Released:** May 5, 2017 · **Phase 3** · **Set in:** 2014 (a few months after the first film)

**Story:** After angering a golden-skinned race called the Sovereign (Rocket steals batteries from them), the Guardians are rescued by Ego, a being who reveals he is Peter Quill's father and a living planet. Gamora and her sister Nebula, who were both raised and tortured by Thanos, slowly repair their bond. Quill bonds with Ego until he learns his father planted seeds across the galaxy to take over every world and gave Quill's mother the brain tumor that killed her. Quill's former captor Yondu, of the Ravagers, helps the Guardians destroy Ego, and Quill loses his god-like powers. Yondu sacrifices himself to save Quill, who realizes Yondu was the father who really raised him. The Ravagers give Yondu a hero's funeral.

**Credit scenes (there are five):**
- **Mid-credits 1:** Kraglin practices using Yondu's whistle-controlled arrow and accidentally hits Drax.
- **Mid-credits 2:** Ravager leader Stakar Ogord (Sylvester Stallone) reunites his old team.
- **Mid-credits 3:** Sovereign leader Ayesha creates a new, more powerful being to destroy the Guardians and names him **Adam** (Adam Warlock, who finally appears in *Vol. 3*).
- **Mid-credits 4:** A moody teenage Groot plays video games and talks back to Quill.
- **Post-credits:** Stan Lee is stranded on a moon talking to the cosmic Watchers.

#### 16. Spider-Man: Homecoming (2017)
**Released:** July 7, 2017 · **Phase 3** · **Set in:** fall 2016, a few months after *Civil War* (an on-screen "8 years later" caption is a known mistake)
*(Co-produced by Sony Pictures and Marvel Studios.)*

**Story:** After his thrilling trip to the German airport fight, 15-year-old Peter Parker is back in Queens, New York, waiting for Tony Stark to call him up as an Avenger while fighting small-time crime in a suit Tony built for him. Adrian Toomes, a salvage worker put out of business by the clean-up of the Battle of New York, has become the Vulture, selling weapons made from alien technology. Peter tries to stop him alone, but a disastrous fight on the Staten Island Ferry leads Tony to take away the suit. Peter discovers that Toomes is the father of his date to the homecoming dance, Liz. Wearing his homemade suit, Peter defeats the Vulture and even saves his life. Peter turns down Tony's offer to join the Avengers, and Aunt May walks in just as he takes off his mask.

**Credit scenes:**
- **Mid-credits:** In prison, Toomes meets criminal Mac Gargan, who asks if he knows Spider-Man's identity. Toomes says no, protecting Peter. *(Gargan finally becomes the Scorpion in *Spider-Man: Brand New Day*.)*
- **Post-credits:** A joke "patience" video starring Captain America that mocks waiting for post-credit scenes.

---

#### 17. Thor: Ragnarok (2017)
**Released:** November 3, 2017 · **Phase 3** · **Set in:** 2017

**Story:** Thor returns to Asgard, exposes Loki's impersonation of Odin, and, with Doctor Strange's help, finds their father in Norway, where Odin dies and frees his first-born daughter, Hela, the goddess of death. Hela destroys Mjolnir and takes over Asgard, while Thor is thrown onto the junk planet Sakaar, ruled by the eccentric Grandmaster. Thor is forced into gladiator fights and faces his champion, the Hulk, who has lived on Sakaar since *Age of Ultron*, and meets Valkyrie, a former Asgardian warrior. Thor, Loki, Hulk and Valkyrie escape, return to Asgard, and realize the only way to stop Hela is to let Surtur, a fire demon, destroy Asgard in "Ragnarok" (the end of Asgard). Thor, having lost an eye, becomes king and leads the surviving Asgardians onto a refugee ship bound for Earth.

**Credit scenes:**
- **Mid-credits:** Thor and Loki talk about whether going to Earth is wise, when a massive ship (Thanos's ship, the Sanctuary II) appears before them. *(This leads directly into the opening of *Avengers: Infinity War*. Note: before leaving Asgard, Loki secretly took the Tesseract.)*
- **Post-credits:** The Grandmaster survives the uprising on Sakaar and declares the revolution "a tie."

---

#### 18. Black Panther (2018)
**Released:** February 16, 2018 · **Phase 3** · **Set in:** 2016 (one week after *Civil War*)

**Story:** T'Challa returns home to Wakanda, a secret, technologically advanced African nation built on a precious metal called vibranium, to be crowned king. With his sister Shuri, the general Okoye, and spy Nakia, he hunts arms dealer Ulysses Klaue in South Korea and meets CIA agent Everett Ross. American former soldier Erik "Killmonger" Stevens arrives, revealing he is T'Challa's cousin, abandoned in Oakland by T'Challa's father after that father killed Erik's father. Killmonger defeats T'Challa in ritual combat, takes the throne, and plans to send Wakandan weapons to oppressed people around the world. T'Challa survives, returns, and defeats Killmonger, who chooses death over imprisonment. Moved by what Killmonger stood for, T'Challa decides Wakanda will open itself to the world and share its knowledge.

**Credit scenes:**
- **Mid-credits:** T'Challa addresses the United Nations and announces that Wakanda will share its technology with the world.
- **Post-credits:** Bucky Barnes wakes up in Wakanda, cured, and the local children call him "White Wolf."

---

#### 19. Avengers: Infinity War (2018)
**Released:** April 27, 2018 · **Phase 3** · **Set in:** 2018

**Story:** Thanos sets out to collect all six Infinity Stones so he can wipe out half of all life in the universe, which he believes will save it from overpopulation. He attacks the Asgardian refugee ship, kills Loki and Heimdall, beats the Hulk, and takes the Space Stone from the Tesseract (Banner crash lands on Earth to warn the heroes). The divided Avengers fight on several fronts: Tony, Doctor Strange and Spider-Man end up on Thanos's devastated home planet Titan with the Guardians; Thor, Rocket and Groot forge a new god-killing axe, Stormbreaker; and Steve's fugitive team protects Vision in Wakanda with Black Panther's army. Thanos sacrifices Gamora to get the Soul Stone, Strange gives up the Time Stone to save Tony's life, and Thanos takes the Mind Stone from Vision's head, killing him. (Thanos also took the Power Stone from Xandar off-screen and the Reality Stone from the Collector.) Thor strikes Thanos with Stormbreaker but does not "go for the head," and Thanos **snaps his fingers**. Half of all life turns to dust, including Spider-Man, Black Panther, Bucky, Doctor Strange, Scarlet Witch, Falcon and most of the Guardians, and Thanos retires to a quiet farm planet.

**Credit scenes:**
- **Post-credits:** As people turn to dust around him, Nick Fury sends an emergency signal on a pager just before he turns to dust himself. The pager shows the symbol of **Captain Marvel**. The credits end with "Thanos will return."

---

#### 20. Ant-Man and the Wasp (2018)
**Released:** July 6, 2018 · **Phase 3** · **Set in:** 2018 (before and during the events of *Infinity War*)

**Story:** Scott Lang is days away from the end of two years of house arrest for helping Captain America in *Civil War*, when Hank Pym and Hope van Dyne (now the Wasp) recruit him because they believe Janet van Dyne is alive in the Quantum Realm and can be reached. They are chased by black-market dealer Sonny Burch and by Ghost (Ava Starr), a woman whose body is unstable after a lab accident and who wants to drain Janet's quantum energy to heal herself. Hank travels into the Quantum Realm and brings Janet back, and Janet uses her new quantum abilities to partly heal Ava. Scott makes it home just before his house arrest ends.

**Credit scenes:**
- **Mid-credits:** Scott goes into the Quantum Realm to collect healing particles for Ava. At that moment, Thanos's snap happens: Hank, Hope and Janet turn to dust, leaving Scott stranded in the Quantum Realm.
- **Post-credits:** A giant ant plays the drums in Scott's empty house as an emergency broadcast plays on the television. Text reads: "Ant-Man and the Wasp will return?"

---

#### 21. Captain Marvel (2019)
**Released:** March 8, 2019 · **Phase 3** · **Set in:** 1995 *(the earliest-set film after Captain America's war years)*

**Story:** Vers is a member of an elite Kree military team with no memory of her past and the ability to shoot energy from her hands. ## 1.5 Sub-Timeline: Spider-Man (Every Spider-Man Film, Every Universe)

Spider-Man's film rights belong to Sony Pictures. That is why there are three different live-action Spider-Men, each in his own universe, and why Tom Holland's films are co-productions between Sony and Marvel Studios. *Spider-Man: No Way Home* brought all three together, so the earlier films are now part of the main story.

| Spider-Man | Actor | Films | Universe number |
|---|---|---|---|
| The original | Tobey Maguire | 2002, 2004, 2007 (directed by Sam Raimi), plus *No Way Home* | Earth-96283 |
| The "Amazing" one | Andrew Garfield | 2012, 2014 (directed by Marc Webb), plus *No Way Home* | Earth-120703 |
| The Avengers' one | Tom Holland | *Civil War* onward | Earth-616 (the main universe; sometimes listed as Earth-199999) |
| Miles Morales and many others | Shameik Moore (voice) | The animated *Spider-Verse* films | Earth-1610 and many more |

---

### The Sam Raimi Trilogy (Tobey Maguire)

---

#### Spider-Man (2002)
**Released:** May 3, 2002 · **Universe:** Earth-96283

**Story:** Shy high school senior Peter Parker is bitten by a genetically altered spider on a school trip and gains spider powers, including the ability to shoot webs naturally from his wrists. He uses his powers to win money in a wrestling match and lets a robber escape out of spite; the same robber later kills his beloved Uncle Ben, teaching Peter that "with great power comes great responsibility." Meanwhile, Norman Osborn, the father of Peter's best friend Harry, tests an unstable performance-enhancing formula on himself and becomes the insane Green Goblin. The Goblin learns Spider-Man's identity and threatens Mary Jane Watson, the girl next door Peter has loved for years, and Aunt May. In the final fight, Norman tries to impale Spider-Man with his glider but is killed by it instead; Harry blames Spider-Man for his father's death. Peter tells Mary Jane they can only be friends, to protect her.

**Credit scenes:** None.

---

#### Spider-Man 2 (2004)
**Released:** June 30, 2004 · **Universe:** Earth-96283

**Story:** Two years later, Peter is exhausted from juggling college, jobs and crime-fighting, and his powers start to fail as he doubts himself. His scientific hero Otto Octavius loses his wife in a failed fusion experiment and is fused to four mechanical arms whose built-in artificial intelligence pushes him to rebuild the reactor, becoming Doctor Octopus. Peter gives up being Spider-Man for a while, but returns when he sees the city needs him. In a famous fight on an elevated train, Spider-Man stops the runaway train with his body, and the passengers see his face and protect him. Doctor Octopus delivers Spider-Man to Harry, who unmasks him; Peter convinces Otto to drown his reactor, and Otto dies doing so. Mary Jane learns Peter's secret and leaves her own wedding to be with him, while Harry finds his father's hidden Green Goblin lair.

**Credit scenes:** None.

---

#### Spider-Man 3 (2007)
**Released:** May 4, 2007 · **Universe:** Earth-96283

**Story:** An alien goo, the black symbiote, arrives on a meteorite and bonds with Peter, giving him a black suit that makes him stronger but also more aggressive and arrogant. Escaped convict Flint Marko falls into a particle experiment and becomes the Sandman; Peter learns Marko was the real killer of Uncle Ben. Harry, now the New Goblin, attacks Peter, loses his memory, then recovers it and manipulates Mary Jane into breaking up with Peter. Peter rips off the symbiote in a church bell tower, and it falls onto rival photographer Eddie Brock, creating **Venom**. Harry and Peter team up to fight Venom and Sandman; Harry dies saving Peter, Eddie Brock dies along with the symbiote when Peter destroys it, and Peter forgives Sandman.

**Credit scenes:** None.

---

### The Marc Webb Films (Andrew Garfield)

---

#### The Amazing Spider-Man (2012)
**Released:** July 3, 2012 · **Universe:** Earth-120703

**Story:** A reboot. Peter Parker, abandoned as a child by his scientist parents and raised by Uncle Ben and Aunt May, finds his father's old research and visits Oscorp, where he is bitten by a genetically engineered spider. After Uncle Ben is killed by a thief Peter let escape, Peter hunts for the killer as Spider-Man, building his own mechanical web-shooters. His father's former partner, Dr. Curt Connors, injects himself with a lizard formula to regrow his missing arm and becomes the Lizard, who plans to turn all of New York into reptiles. Peter falls for classmate Gwen Stacy, whose father, Police Captain George Stacy, dies helping stop the Lizard, and makes Peter promise to keep Gwen out of danger. Peter at first breaks off the relationship, then hints that he may not keep that promise.

**Credit scenes:**
- **Mid-credits:** In his prison cell, Connors is visited by a man in shadow who asks whether he told Peter the truth about his father. Connors says no and tells him to leave Peter alone. *(The man was later revealed as Gustav Fiers, but the planned sequels that would have explained him were cancelled.)*

---

#### The Amazing Spider-Man 2 (2014)
**Released:** May 2, 2014 · **Universe:** Earth-120703

**Story:** Peter is happily dating Gwen despite his promise to her late father. His childhood friend Harry Osborn returns to run Oscorp and learns he is dying of the same genetic disease that killed his father Norman, and believes Spider-Man's blood can save him. Max Dillon, a lonely Oscorp electrician who idolizes Spider-Man, falls into a tank of electric eels and becomes Electro. Peter learns his father fled because he refused to let Oscorp misuse his spider research. Harry injects himself with spider venom, becomes the Green Goblin, and frees Electro; Spider-Man defeats Electro at a power plant, but in the clock tower fight with the Goblin, Gwen falls and her neck snaps as Peter's web catches her, killing her. Months later, a grieving Peter returns to face a new villain, the Rhino, after watching Gwen's graduation speech about hope. Meanwhile, the shadowy Gustav Fiers visits the imprisoned Harry to start assembling a villain team, hinting at the Sinister Six.

**Credit scenes:**
- **Mid-credits:** A clip from *X-Men: Days of Future Past* (a cross-promotion; it has no story connection to Spider-Man).

> Sony's planned sequels and a "Sinister Six" film were cancelled after this film, which led to the deal with Marvel Studios and Tom Holland's version. Garfield's Peter finally got a happier ending in *No Way Home*, where he saves MJ from a fall the way he failed to save Gwen.

### Tom Holland's Spider-Man (the main universe)

These films already have full entries in section 1.1. Below, each one is summarized again from Peter Parker's point of view, so you can follow his personal story on its own.

---

#### Captain America: Civil War (2016): Peter's first appearance
**Released:** May 6, 2016 · see entry #13

**Peter's story:** Tony Stark visits 15-year-old Peter Parker in his Queens apartment, reveals he knows Peter is the masked "Spider-Man" from online videos, and recruits him for his side of the Avengers' split. At the German airport battle, Peter grabs Captain America's shield, webs up Bucky and Falcon, and helps bring down Ant-Man in his giant form. Tony sends him home with a new high-tech suit, telling him to stay a "friendly neighborhood Spider-Man" for now. **Credit scenes:** the post-credits scene shows Peter discovering his new web-shooter projects a red Spider-Man symbol.

---

#### Spider-Man: Homecoming (2017): Peter wants to be an Avenger
**Released:** July 7, 2017 · see entry #16

**Peter's story:** Peter is desperate to prove himself to Tony, who keeps him at arm's length through his bodyguard Happy Hogan. He takes on the Vulture, Adrian Toomes, without permission, nearly sinks the Staten Island Ferry and loses his Stark suit, learning that he has to be a hero without the gadgets ("If you're nothing without the suit, then you shouldn't have it"). He stops Toomes in his homemade suit and turns down a place in the Avengers. He gains a best friend and confidant, Ned, and a future love interest, Michelle, who goes by "MJ." **Credit scenes:** in prison, Toomes hides Peter's identity from Mac Gargan (mid-credits); a Captain America "patience" video (post-credits).

---

#### Avengers: Infinity War (2018) and Avengers: Endgame (2019): Peter as an Avenger
**Released:** April 27, 2018 and April 26, 2019 · see entries #19 and #22

**Peter's story:** Peter jumps off his school bus to help Tony fight Thanos's henchman Ebony Maw, is given the Iron Spider armor, and is "knighted" as an Avenger on the planet Titan. When Thanos snaps, Peter turns to dust in Tony's arms ("I don't feel so good"). Five years later, he returns through a portal for the final battle, carries the Infinity Gauntlet across the battlefield, and is beside Tony when he dies. **Credit scenes:** *Infinity War* ends with Nick Fury paging Captain Marvel; *Endgame* has none.

---

#### Spider-Man: Far From Home (2019): Peter without Tony
**Released:** July 2, 2019 · see entry #23

**Peter's story:** Grieving his mentor and pressured by a world asking who the next Iron Man will be, Peter just wants a school trip to Europe with MJ. He hands Tony's powerful EDITH glasses to Mysterio, believing him a worthy hero, and learns too late that Mysterio is a fraud. He takes responsibility, defeats Mysterio, and starts dating MJ. **Credit scenes:** J. Jonah Jameson reveals Peter's identity to the world (mid-credits); Nick Fury and Maria Hill were Skrulls in disguise (post-credits).

---

#### Spider-Man: No Way Home (2021): Peter loses everything
**Released:** December 17, 2021 · see entry #27

**Peter's story:** Unmasked and hounded, Peter asks Doctor Strange to make the world forget, which pulls villains (and two other Spider-Men) in from other universes. Instead of sending the villains home to die, Peter chooses to cure them, and pays for it when the Green Goblin kills Aunt May. With help from the other two Peter Parkers, who share their own losses (Uncle Ben and Gwen Stacy), he cures the villains and sacrifices his own identity to save the multiverse: nobody, including MJ and Ned, remembers him anymore. This is the moment he truly becomes the Spider-Man of the comics: alone, broke, and still doing the right thing. **Credit scenes:** Eddie Brock leaves a piece of the Venom symbiote behind (mid-credits); a teaser for *Multiverse of Madness* (post-credits).

---

#### Spider-Man: Brand New Day (2026): Peter on his own
**Released:** July 31, 2026 · see entry #38

**Peter's story:** Four years after being forgotten, Peter is a full-time street-level hero with no mentor, no friends and no help, which makes him more isolated and more reckless. His powers begin mutating, a sign there is more to his spider bite than he knew, and he needs Bruce Banner's help to keep them in check. Caught between the Punisher's brutal methods and a grieving mutant, Jean Grey, he chooses mercy and nearly dies for it. He ends the film deciding he no longer has to carry everything by himself, and reconnects with Ned (MJ still does not remember him). **Credit scenes:** no mid-credits scene; the post-credits scene shows Ned's Spidey Tracker app finding Spider-Man somewhere in deep space.

### The Animated Spider-Verse (Miles Morales)

These Sony animated films are their own story, separate from the Avengers films, but they explain the multiverse beautifully and nod to the live-action films.

---

#### Spider-Man: Into the Spider-Verse (2018)
**Released:** December 14, 2018 · **Universe:** Earth-1610 (Miles's world)

**Story:** Brooklyn teenager Miles Morales is bitten by a radioactive spider and then watches his world's Spider-Man, Peter Parker, die trying to stop the crime boss Kingpin, whose particle collider is tearing holes between universes. The collider pulls in Spider-People from other worlds: a tired, older Peter B. Parker, Gwen Stacy (Spider-Woman), Spider-Man Noir, Peni Parker with her robot, and Spider-Ham. Miles discovers his beloved Uncle Aaron is the villain Prowler, who is killed by Kingpin after refusing to kill Miles. Miles takes his "leap of faith," masters his powers, sends the others home and destroys the collider. He becomes his world's new Spider-Man.

**Credit scenes:**
- **Post-credits:** Miguel O'Hara, Spider-Man 2099 (Oscar Isaac), gets his multiverse-travel watch working and visits the world of the 1967 Spider-Man cartoon, recreating the famous "pointing Spider-Men" meme. A tribute to Spider-Man's creators Stan Lee and Steve Ditko also plays.

---

#### Spider-Man: Across the Spider-Verse (2023)
**Released:** June 2, 2023 · **Universe:** many

**Story:** A year later, Miles's minor villain the Spot, who can open holes between universes, grows dangerously powerful. Gwen, now a member of Miguel O'Hara's Spider-Society (an organization of Spider-People from across the multiverse), visits Miles, and Miles follows her into other worlds. Miguel reveals "canon events," tragic moments every Spider-Person must live through to keep their universe stable, such as the death of a police captain close to them, and Miles learns his own father is about to die. Refusing to accept that, Miles flees from hundreds of Spider-People, but is sent to Earth-42, the world his spider came from, where he never got powers and his alternate self is the Prowler. The film ends on a cliffhanger as Gwen assembles a team to rescue him. *(It includes live-action clips of Tobey Maguire and Andrew Garfield, a line blaming "Doctor Strange and the little nerd" for multiverse damage (meaning Holland's Peter), Donald Glover as a live-action Prowler, and a cameo by Mrs. Chen from the Venom films.)*

**Credit scenes:** None.

---

#### Spider-Man: Beyond the Spider-Verse (coming June 18, 2027)
**Release date:** June 18, 2027 (not yet released)

**What is known:** The conclusion of the animated trilogy, picking up from *Across the Spider-Verse*'s cliffhanger with Miles trapped on Earth-42 and Gwen's team on the way. The plot and credit scenes have not been revealed.

## 1.6 Sub-Timeline: Venom and Sony's Spider-Man Universe

Sony Pictures made six films about Spider-Man's villains and side characters, without Spider-Man himself. They take place in their own universe, which *Across the Spider-Verse* labels **Earth-688**. Only the Venom films and *Morbius* actually cross over with the main Avengers story; *Madame Web* and *Kraven the Hunter* are fully standalone.

> **Status (November 2026):** Sony has paused this series. Sony Pictures chief Tom Rothman said in August 2026 that no films are in active development, though he has also talked about a future "fresh reboot." Treat these six films as a finished set.

| Must-watch for the main story? | Film |
|---|---|
| Yes (sets up the *No Way Home* credit scene) | *Venom: Let There Be Carnage* |
| Helpful | *Venom*, *Venom: The Last Dance* |
| Optional (one credit-scene link only) | *Morbius* |
| Fully optional | *Madame Web*, *Kraven the Hunter* |

---

#### Venom (2018)
**Released:** October 5, 2018 · **Universe:** Earth-688

**Story:** Eddie Brock, a reporter in San Francisco, loses his job and his fiancée Anne Weying after confronting Carlton Drake, the head of the Life Foundation, about deadly human experiments. Drake's company has brought back alien life forms called symbiotes from space, and they need human hosts to survive. Eddie sneaks into the lab and bonds with a symbiote named Venom, which gives him super strength, healing and a voice in his head with a craving for eating heads. Drake bonds with Riot, the symbiotes' leader, who plans to fly a rocket back to space and bring an invasion fleet to Earth. Venom decides he likes Earth and Eddie, and the two destroy Riot's rocket. Eddie and Venom stay together, agreeing to eat only "bad guys."

**Credit scenes:**
- **Mid-credits:** Eddie interviews imprisoned serial killer Cletus Kasady (Woody Harrelson) at San Quentin prison, who promises "there's gonna be carnage."
- **Post-credits:** An extended clip from the animated film *Spider-Man: Into the Spider-Verse*.

---

#### Venom: Let There Be Carnage (2021)
**Released:** October 1, 2021 · **Universe:** Earth-688

**Story:** Eddie's interviews with Cletus Kasady help police find his victims' bodies, sending Kasady to death row. At their last meeting, Kasady bites Eddie and swallows a piece of the symbiote, which grows into a new, more violent symbiote: **Carnage**. Meanwhile, Eddie and Venom fight like an old married couple and split up. Kasady escapes and frees his childhood love Frances Barrison (Shriek), whose sonic scream can hurt symbiotes. Eddie and Venom make up and stop Carnage and Shriek at a church on the night of the couple's wedding; Venom eats Carnage, and Shriek dies. Eddie and Venom hide out on a beach in Mexico.

**Credit scenes:**
- **Mid-credits:** In a Mexican hotel room, Venom starts telling Eddie about the knowledge his kind shares across universes, when a flash of light suddenly transports them into the main Marvel universe. On the television, J. Jonah Jameson is revealing that Peter Parker (Tom Holland) is Spider-Man. *(This leads directly into the mid-credits scene of* Spider-Man: No Way Home*.)*

---

#### Morbius (2022)
**Released:** April 1, 2022 · **Universe:** Earth-688

**Story:** Dr. Michael Morbius, a brilliant scientist born with a rare, deadly blood disease, tries to cure himself with the DNA of vampire bats. It works too well: he gains super strength, flight, echolocation and an uncontrollable thirst for human blood. His adoptive brother Milo, who has the same illness, steals the formula and becomes a murderous vampire who enjoys his new power. Milo kills Morbius's colleague and love interest Martine, who bites Morbius as she dies. Morbius kills Milo with an antidote and flies off with a swarm of bats, now a fugitive.

**Credit scenes:**
- **Mid-credits 1:** The sky glows purple, and Adrian Toomes, the Vulture from *Spider-Man: Homecoming* (Michael Keaton), appears in a prison cell in Morbius's world, pulled there by the side effects of Doctor Strange's spell in *No Way Home*. He is soon released.
- **Mid-credits 2:** Toomes, wearing a new Vulture suit, finds Morbius and suggests they team up. *(This was meant to set up a "Sinister Six" team of villains, which was never made, and no later film explains it.)*

---

#### Madame Web (2024)
**Released:** February 14, 2024 · **Universe:** Earth-688 · **Set in:** 2003

**Story:** Cassandra "Cassie" Webb, a paramedic in New York, nearly drowns and begins having visions of the future. She learns that her mother was researching rare spiders in the Amazon when she was killed by Ezekiel Sims, a man who stole the research and gained spider powers. Ezekiel has visions of being killed by three young Spider-Women and is hunting the teenage girls who will become them: Julia Cornwall, Anya Corazon and Mattie Franklin. Cassie protects the girls, and Ezekiel is killed by a falling Pepsi sign during the final fight. Cassie is left blind and paralyzed but with much stronger psychic powers, becoming Madame Web. *(Ben Parker appears, and Peter Parker's birth happens off-screen, but there is no Spider-Man and no link to any other film.)*

**Credit scenes:** None.

---

#### Venom: The Last Dance (2024)
**Released:** October 25, 2024 · **Universe:** Earth-688

**Story:** Back in their own universe after *No Way Home*, Eddie and Venom are wanted by the police and by the military, led by General Strickland of the secret base Area 55. Worse, Knull, the dark god who created the symbiotes and is imprisoned in the void, is sending monsters called Xenophages to find the "Codex," a key that only exists while Eddie and Venom are bonded together. # Part 2: The Shows

Starting in 2021, Marvel Studios began telling parts of the main story in Disney+ shows. Some of them are essential (*WandaVision* sets up *Multiverse of Madness*; *Loki* sets up the entire Multiverse Saga), and others are side stories you can skip without being lost.

**How to read the "Importance" rating:**
- ★★★ **Essential**: the films assume you have seen it.
- ★★ **Recommended**: introduces characters who return in films.
- ★ **Optional**: a self-contained side story.

From 2015 to 2019, Marvel Television made six connected shows for Netflix, set in New York's streets, which fans call the "Defenders Saga." For years nobody knew if they counted. Then Charlie Cox's Matt Murdock appeared in *Spider-Man: No Way Home*, Vincent D'Onofrio's Kingpin appeared in *Hawkeye*, and *Daredevil: Born Again* was rebuilt as a direct sequel to the Netflix *Daredevil*. In January 2024 Disney+ added all six shows to its official timeline, and in March 2024 Marvel confirmed that they are canon.

# Part 3: The X-Men and the Mutant Story

**What are mutants?** People born with a genetic change (the "X-gene") that gives them powers, usually appearing at puberty. They are feared and hated by much of humanity. Professor Charles Xavier, a powerful telepath (mind reader), runs a school that trains young mutants to protect a world that fears them: the **X-Men**. His old friend Erik Lehnsherr, **Magneto**, who controls metal, believes mutants must fight humans before humans destroy them.

**Why are they separate?** Before Disney bought 20th Century Fox in 2019, Fox owned the film rights to the X-Men (and the Fantastic Four). Fox's films (2000 to 2020) are their own universe, now officially numbered **Earth-10005**. Since the purchase, Marvel Studios has been bringing mutants into the main story, and the original Fox X-Men return in *Avengers: Doomsday*.

**The two timelines inside the Fox films:**
- **Original timeline:** *X-Men*, *X2*, *The Last Stand*, *Origins: Wolverine*, *The Wolverine*, and the future scenes of *Days of Future Past*.
- **Revised timeline:** In *Days of Future Past*, Wolverine travels back to 1973 and changes history. *Apocalypse*, *Dark Phoenix* and the *Deadpool* films take place in this new timeline, where the events of *The Last Stand* never happened.
- *X-Men: First Class* takes place before the split, so it belongs to both.

#### 1. X-Men (2000)
**Released:** July 14, 2000 · **Set in:** "the not too distant future" (with a 1944 opening at the Auschwitz concentration camp) · **Timeline:** original

**Story:** A young Erik Lehnsherr's power over metal first appears when he is separated from his parents at Auschwitz. Decades later, teenage runaway Rogue, whose touch drains the life and powers of anyone she touches, meets Logan, known as **Wolverine**, a gruff loner with healing powers, metal claws and no memory of his past. They are rescued from Magneto's henchman Sabretooth by Storm and Cyclops and taken to Professor Xavier's school, where Logan meets Jean Grey. Magneto and his Brotherhood of Mutants (Mystique, Toad, Sabretooth) plan to use a machine to turn the world's leaders, gathered on Ellis Island, into mutants, powered by Rogue's ability to absorb his power, though the change is unstable and kills the anti-mutant Senator Kelly. The X-Men stop the machine atop the Statue of Liberty, and Logan saves the dying Rogue by letting her absorb his healing. Magneto is imprisoned in a plastic cell, where Xavier visits him to play chess, and Logan leaves to search for his past at Alkali Lake.

#### 2. X2 (2003)
**Released:** May 2, 2003 · **Timeline:** original
*(Also known as* X2: X-Men United*.)*

**Story:** A mind-controlled teleporting mutant, Nightcrawler, attacks the President in the White House, giving military scientist Colonel William Stryker an excuse to raid Xavier's school and capture the Professor. Storm and Nightcrawler free Xavier just in time, and Logan kills Stryker's assassin Lady Deathstrike and leaves Stryker to drown. Magneto leads an army of mutants against the cure facility on Alcatraz Island, and the X-Men, led by Storm and Wolverine, fight to stop him; Magneto is stripped of his powers by a cure dart. # Part 4: The Fantastic Four

**Who they are:** Marvel's first family, created in 1961, the comic that launched the modern Marvel age. A cosmic ray accident gives four people powers:

| Hero | Real name | Power |
|---|---|---|
| Mister Fantastic | Reed Richards | Stretches his body; the smartest man alive |
| Invisible Woman | Sue Storm | Turns invisible and creates force fields |
| Human Torch | Johnny Storm (Sue's brother) | Bursts into flame and flies ("Flame on!") |
| The Thing | Ben Grimm (Reed's best friend) | A super-strong body of orange rock ("It's clobberin' time!") |

Their greatest enemy is **Doctor Doom** (Victor von Doom), the genius dictator of a small European country, Latveria, and their most terrifying threat is **Galactus**, a giant being who eats planets, along with his herald, the **Silver Surfer**.

Like the X-Men, the Fantastic Four's film rights belonged to 20th Century Fox until Disney bought it in 2019, which is why there are three separate versions before Marvel Studios made its own.

---

## 4.1 Every Fantastic Four Film, in Release Order

---

#### 1. The Fantastic Four (1994): never officially released
**Made:** 1992 to 1994 · **Status:** never released; known only from bootleg copies

**Story:** A German producer, Bernd Eichinger, held the film rights and had to start making a film before they expired at the end of 1992, so he made a very cheap version (about $1 million) with low-budget producer Roger Corman. College friends Reed Richards and Victor von Doom run an experiment with a passing comet that goes wrong, and Victor is believed dead. Ten years later, Reed, Sue, Johnny and Ben fly into space to study the comet, but a jewel thief called the Jeweler has swapped the diamond protecting them for a fake, so they are exposed to cosmic radiation and gain powers. Doom, alive and in armor, captures them, and the Jeweler kidnaps Ben's love interest, the blind sculptor Alicia Masters. The team escapes, Johnny redirects Doom's laser away from New York, and Reed and Sue marry. The planned premiere was cancelled, and Marvel's Avi Arad later said he bought the film so it would never be shown; the full story is told in the documentary *Doomed!* (2015).

**Credit scenes:** None.

---

#### 2. Fantastic Four (2005)
**Released:** July 8, 2005 · **Universe:** its own (Fox)

**Story:** Struggling scientist Reed Richards and his friend, astronaut Ben Grimm, ask Reed's former college rival, billionaire Victor von Doom, to fund a trip to Doom's space station to study a cosmic storm, joined by Reed's ex-girlfriend Sue Storm (now Doom's employee) and her brother Johnny, the pilot. The storm arrives early and gives all four powers, and Doom, too, slowly turns into living metal with electrical powers. The team becomes famous after a rescue on the Brooklyn Bridge, but Ben, turned into a rock-covered monster, loses his fiancée and suffers. Doom manipulates Ben into using Reed's machine to turn human, then captures Reed and attacks the team's headquarters, the Baxter Building. Ben turns back into the Thing to help, and the four defeat Doom together by heating him with Johnny's flames and then freezing him with water. Doom's frozen body is shipped home to Latveria, Reed proposes to Sue, and Ben starts dating Alicia Masters. *(Johnny Storm is played by Chris Evans, who went on to play Captain America and played Johnny again in* Deadpool & Wolverine*.)*

**Credit scenes:** None.

---

#### 3. Fantastic Four: Rise of the Silver Surfer (2007)
**Released:** June 15, 2007 · **Universe:** same as the 2005 film

**Story:** As Reed and Sue prepare for their wedding, a mysterious silver figure on a flying surfboard races around the world, leaving giant craters and draining London's River Thames. Johnny chases the **Silver Surfer**, and a touch from him makes Johnny swap powers with anyone he touches. The Surfer's energy also frees and heals Doctor Doom, who teams up with the United States military, captures the Surfer and steals his board, which turns out to be a beacon for **Galactus**, shown here as a giant cosmic cloud that devours planets. The Fantastic Four defeat Doom with Johnny absorbing all four powers at once, and return the board to the Surfer, who turns against his master and sacrifices himself to destroy Galactus. Reed and Sue finally marry in Japan.

**Credit scenes:**
- **Mid-credits:** The Silver Surfer's seemingly lifeless body floats in space, his eyes open, and his board flies back to him.

---

#### 4. Fantastic Four (2015)
**Released:** August 7, 2015 · **Universe:** its own (Fox reboot)
*(Often styled* Fant4stic*.)*

**Story:** A darker reboot. Reed Richards and his best friend Ben Grimm have been building a teleporter since childhood. Reed is recruited by Dr. Franklin Storm and his children, Sue and Johnny, to build a "Quantum Gate" to another dimension with a bitter young genius, Victor von Doom. After a drunken late-night decision, Reed, Johnny, Victor and Ben secretly travel to the alien world, which they call Planet Zero; Victor is lost there, and the trip changes the others' bodies (Sue is affected on the way back). A year later, the government uses them as weapons, and Victor returns fused to his suit with powerful mental abilities, kills many people (including Franklin Storm), and tries to destroy Earth using Planet Zero's energy. The four work together to defeat him and set up a new headquarters, naming themselves the Fantastic Four. *(The director, Josh Trank, publicly disowned the version the studio released.)*

**Credit scenes:** None.

---

#### 5. The Fantastic Four: First Steps (2025): the Marvel Studios version
**Released:** July 25, 2025 · **Universe:** Earth-828 · see entry #37 in Part 1

**The family's story:** In a bright, retro-futuristic version of 1964 on their own Earth (Earth-828), the Fantastic Four (Pedro Pascal as Reed, Vanessa Kirby as Sue, Joseph Quinn as Johnny, Ebon Moss-Bachrach as Ben) have been beloved heroes for four years, with their own robot helper H.E.R.B.I.E. and their own cartoon show. Sue's pregnancy turns the film into a story about parenthood: when Galactus offers to spare Earth only in exchange for their newborn son Franklin, Reed and Sue refuse, and the world turns on them. Johnny reaches the Silver Surfer, Shalla-Bal, by learning her history, and Ben helps the Mole Man shelter New York's people underground. Sue nearly dies holding back Galactus while the Surfer pushes him through a portal, and baby Franklin brings his mother back to life, revealing that he has incredible cosmic powers. **Credit scenes:** four years later, Sue finds young Franklin with Doctor Doom (mid-credits); H.E.R.B.I.E. switches off the old Fantastic Four cartoon (post-credits).
